KSU Salina Showcase

July 31, 2026

My marketing intern coworker, Lupita, presented my last project as a student intern at KSU Salina to showcase the college using a trending audio. Decided to use the Clams Casino audio that has over 100k vids within TikTok. All the videos were in Dropbox, with years of footage that I could peruse, and I included all other sound effects and overlays. Initially started with a storyboard in Photoshop to show my process of ideas I wanted to explore with my coworker. Every editing element was done in After Effects.

Stellar Vince - Left Alone (Lyric Visualizer)

Feb 2, 2026

Another project with my good friend, Stellar Vince, to create a lyric visualizer. A different approach was to start with Photoshop to make my initial point, and then continue editing. Although the process was similar to Time, it incorporated more elements of cosmic atmosphere, with a nebula overlay and Vince as a silhouette. Also tried more text-based approaches to simulate echoes and reverb, and there are a few more sequences of sound effects in the intro and outro. Programs used for Left Alone were Photoshop, After Effects, and Media Encoder.

ALI Center & Library Interviews

October 3, 2025

During my time in college, we had major renovations. One of the renovations was the library, which received a complete overhaul to include more study rooms, group rooms, offices, a recording studio, and private study spaces. Everything was recorded by the media and communications team. Editing, captions, and graphics were done in Premiere Pro. We used Lytho and Frame.io to check proofs, so this is the second version of the project.

Stellar Vince - Time (Official Visualizer)

March 26, 2025

A project my childhood friend and I created to build a lyric visualizer. Stellar Vince is the writer, producer, and singer of the song. I did all the directing, editing, motion graphics, rendering, and media compiling. When starting on a project, I like to browse mood boards on Cosmos. Whether it's a photo, a video shoot, a design, an overlay, or a 3D animation piece, I use it as inspiration to fit the project. Time was completed in under two weeks, during which I finished most of it while on vacation in Mexico City, with its beautiful architecture and compelling design. Every component of editing and motion graphics was done in After Effects, then compiled for media upload using Media Encoder. The video has amassed 28k views.
